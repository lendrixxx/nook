Nook Privacy Policy

Nook helps users manage their personal schedules and tasks.

Google Calendar Access

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Nook requests read-only access to your Google Calendar data to display your calendar events inside the app.

Nook does not sell, share, or transfer your Google Calendar data to third parties. Calendar data is only used to provide calendar integration features.

Data Storage

Authentication information and calendar data are used only for providing the requested features. You may disconnect Google Calendar access at any time through your Google Account settings.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer through the Nook project repository.
