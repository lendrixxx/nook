Nook — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Nook is a personal, single-developer weather and to-do companion app. This page explains what data it touches and why.

What Nook stores, and where

  • On your device only (browser local storage, never sent anywhere): your to-do list, your chosen character, and your app preferences.
  • Weather data: your location is sent to the Open-Meteo weather API to fetch current conditions. It is not stored by Nook beyond the current session.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Nook requests read-only access (calendar.readonly) — it cannot create, edit, or delete anything in your calendar.

The connection works through a small server component ("the Worker") that Nook's developer operates:

  • Google issues a refresh token after you consent. This is stored server-side, associated only with a random session id — never with your name or email.
  • Your browser only ever holds that random session id, not any Google token.
  • Calendar event data (titles, times) is fetched on request to display today's schedule in the app, and cached locally on your device for offline viewing.
  • No calendar data is shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

You can disconnect at any time from Nook's Settings, which revokes access with Google and deletes the stored session.
